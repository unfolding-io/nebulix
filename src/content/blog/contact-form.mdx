## Getting Your Contact Form Up & Running

The contact form is handled by an [Astro Action](https://docs.astro.build/en/guides/actions/) (`actions.contact`) with providers in `src/lib/forms/`. Multiple topics are supported — each topic can have its own destination email or Slack channel. Open the dialog with a link to `href="#contact"`.

Configure the form in the CMS under **SETTINGS > CONTACT & SOCIAL** (provider: `mailgun`, `postmark`, or `slack`). Set secrets in `.env` locally and in your host’s environment UI for production. `CONTACT_FORM_ENDPOINT` overrides the CMS provider when set. For Mailgun/Postmark, `FROM_EMAIL_ADDRESS` must be an authorized sender on your domain.

On desktop the dialog shows an optional side image from the CMS; on mobile the form is shown alone.

### MailGun

```
CONTACT_FORM_ENDPOINT=mailgun
FROM_EMAIL_ADDRESS=hello@your-domain.com
TO_EMAIL_ADDRESS=hello@your-domain.com
MAILGUN_API_KEY=XXXXXXXXX
MAILGUN_DOMAIN=your-domain.com
MAILGUN_API_URL=https://api.eu.mailgun.net
```

`MAILGUN_API_URL` is `https://api.eu.mailgun.net` (EU) or `https://api.mailgun.net` (US) — do not include `/v3` (the action adds it).

### Postmark

Add your server token to the .env.

```
POSTMARK_SERVER_TOKEN=XXXXXXXXX
```

### Slack

For slack to work you first need to create an app, then add the slack token + channel id to your .env

```
SLACK_CHANNEL_ID=XXXXXXXXX
SLACK_TOKEN=XXXXXXXXX
```

**Create your slack app:**

1. Go to [https://api.slack.com/apps](https://api.slack.com/apps)
1. Click “Create an App” Button.
1. Select “from scratch”
1. Set name and workspace
1. Go to OAuth & Permissions menu.
1. Add Scopes ( chat:write, chat:write:customize, chat:write:public)
1. In the left settings menu select "install app" and connect it to your workspace
1. Now you have your **Bot User OAuth Token** this is your `SLACK_TOKEN`
